Normalized scale and axle positions; separated steering, spinning wheels and stationary brakes; merged compatible meshes; generated three distance levels; compressed geometry with Draco; resized working textures to at most 2048 pixels. The steering wheel is a separate mount (for the in-car camera) and the cabin keeps more detail than the rest of the car (30 % of source triangles instead of 9 %). Runtime adaptations add adjustable paint, calibrated materials, lamps and a simplified shadow caster.

The source download is unchanged. The preparation script is `tools/car-assets/prepare.py`. `geometry.json` records the prepared dimensions and triangle counts. Keep this credit and the CC BY 4.0 notice with redistributions. Vehicle names and marks remain the property of their owners.
