Normalized scale to the real car's 2,896 mm wheelbase and set the axle positions; split each axle's wheel mesh into two spinning wheels (the rear ones keep their driveshafts); made the separate steering wheel its own mount (for the in-car camera); merged compatible meshes; generated three distance levels; compressed geometry with Draco; resized working textures to at most 2048 pixels and stored them as WebP. The livery, cockpit and windscreen keep their full source detail. Runtime adaptations add calibrated materials, a rain light and a simplified shadow caster. The livery is kept as authored, so this car is not repainted in the garage.

The source download is unchanged. The preparation script is `tools/car-assets/prepare.py`. `geometry.json` records the prepared dimensions and triangle counts.
