Adaptations: normalized to a representative 3.6 m wheelbase; separated the four authored wheel assemblies and stationary front tyre deflectors; retained the original UV livery; created three distance levels; tuned body and tyre materials; reduced textures to 2048 pixels and WebP; compressed geometry with Draco. The MCL60 and RB19 retain an animated authored steering assembly. The SF23 uses a small supplemental generic race steering wheel for the cockpit view.

Dimensions are measured from the artwork at this chosen scale, not asserted factory geometry. Performance, power output, suspension and grip are game tuning; the game does not simulate a separate hybrid energy-deployment system or DRS. Source downloads remain unchanged. Rebuild with `tools/car-assets/prepare-modern-f1.py`.
